# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon

## What is this hackathon?

This is a worldwide, online hackathon hosted by Meta. Participants build a project using Meta's AI tools and submit it for judging.

- Hackathon dates: October 26 to November 4, 2026
- Project deadline: November 4, 2026 at 9:00 PM Eastern Time
- Judging: November 4 to 8, 2026; winners announced November 9.
- You have confirmed that you were accepted.

Check the official event page for any updates to these details.

## Your track: Indie Developer

The Indie Developer track is for individual developers and students. This is the right track for you as a solo builder.

## What the judges value

Meta says projects in this track are judged on:

- **Creativity:** Is the idea interesting and thoughtful?
- **Technical ambition:** Does it use technology in a meaningful way?
- **Does it work?:** Can people actually use and try the project?

### Are there score percentages?

The public event page does not give percentages for creativity, technical ambition, whether the project works, or the demo. It does say that the *application review* weighed the project idea and the applicant's technical experience equally. That is about getting accepted, not the score for the finished project. Do not assume the project categories are worth equal percentages unless the official rules say so.

The public page also does not list the exact files or demo format to submit. Check the official rules and submission instructions in the participant event page.

## What kinds of projects fit?

Meta lists two themes:

- **Build for Your World:** Use Meta AI to solve a problem you know from your own life, work, or interests.
- **Push the Frontier:** Use AI to make progress in areas such as software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, or science.

For a solo developer, a small project based on a problem you understand is a practical starting point. Show the problem, show how Meta AI helps, and make the result work.

## What this means for our project

These are practical recommendations based on the judging priorities, not extra official rules:

1. Pick one clear problem to solve.
2. Show how Meta AI helps solve it, rather than adding AI just for appearance.
3. Finish one complete experience that someone can try.
4. Make a short demo that clearly shows the project working.

A small, working project is a better goal than many unfinished features.

## What to check

Read the official rules and submission instructions before submitting. They explain exactly what to provide and any requirements for the project.
